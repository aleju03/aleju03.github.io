Rhythm Keys song and chart credits
==================================

Used by the Rhythm Keys app (src/components/os/games/VsrgApp.tsx). Every
folder here is one track: song.mp3 as it ships in the beatmapset, and
chart.json converted from that set's .osu by scripts/osu-chart.py (long
notes become taps at their head, the game is tap-only).

The music belongs to its artists and the charts to the mappers named below,
both from the osu! community. Nothing here is mine, none of it is sold, and
anyone who wants a track taken down can mail alejimenezu@gmail.com.
